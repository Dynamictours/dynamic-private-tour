Route Itinerary

Day 1-2

Chengdu - Gateway to Western Sichuan

Your adventure begins in Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan Province. Visit the adorable giant pandas at the Chengdu Research Base, explore ancient alleys of Kuanzhai Xiangzi, and savor world-famous Sichuan hotpot. Chengdu is your base for exploring the stunning mountain scenery of western Sichuan.

Two Amazing Mountain Destinations

This route takes you to two of Sichuan's most beautiful alpine destinations: Bipenggou and Siguniang (Four Sisters Mountain). Both are located in Aba Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, offering breathtaking views of snow-capped mountains, pristine lakes, and Tibetan culture!

Where to Stay in Chengdu

  • Chunxi Road / Taikoo Li
    City center, shopping district
  • Kuanzhai Alley Area
    Historic district, local flavor
  • Wuhou District
    Near Wuhou Temple

💡 Start your mountain adventure well-rested. Spend 1-2 days in Chengdu to acclimatize before heading to higher altitudes.

Day 3

Bipenggou - The Turquoise Valley

A spectacular alpine valley known for its crystal-clear turquoise lakes, cascading waterfalls, and snow-capped peaks. Located in Li County, about 200km from Chengdu. Features include the majestic Princess Peak, Napa Lake, and ancient forests. Perfect for photography!

🚗 From Chengdu to Bipenggou

  • 🚗Private Car: 4-5 hours
  • 🚐Group Tour: most convenient option

Highlights of Bipenggou

🏞️ Key Spots: Napa Lake (娜姆湖), Panlong Waterfall (盘羊瀑布), Alpine Grasslands, Princess Peak (女皇峰), Yaks and Tibetan villages

⛰️ Altitude: 2,000-5,000m

Where to Stay

  • Bipenggou Scenic Area
    Inside park, limited options
  • Li County (Lixian)
    30 mins from entrance

💡 Consider staying overnight to fully explore Bipenggou. Bring warm clothing!

Day 4-5

Siguniang (Four Sisters Mountain) - The Alps of Sichuan

One of China's most beautiful mountain ranges, featuring four majestic peaks (Da Feng, Er Feng, San Feng, Yao Mei Feng). Known as the "Oriental Alps," it offers stunning alpine scenery, lush meadows, and challenging hiking trails. A paradise for hikers, photographers, and nature lovers.

🚗 From Bipenggou to Siguniang

  • 🚗Private Car: 3-4 hours (via Dujiangyan)
  • 📅Recommended: Return to Chengdu first, then go to Siguniang

Exploring Siguniang Mountain

🏔️ The Four Sisters:

🌄 Da Feng (5,025m) - Easiest to climb

🌄 Er Feng (5,276m) - Popular for trekking

🌄 San Feng (5,355m) - Technical climbing

🌄 Yao Mei Feng (6,250m) - Highest, most difficult

🚶 Hiking Trails: Changping Valley, Haizi Valley, Shuangqiao Valley

Where to Stay

  • Rilong Town (Rizhao Jinshan)
    Base of mountains
  • Shuangqiao Valley Area
    Near main scenic area
  • Wenchuan County
    1 hour from mountains

💡 Spend 2 days here to experience the best of Siguniang Mountain. Consider hiring a local guide for mountain hikes.

Day 6-7

Return to Chengdu

After your mountain adventure, return to Chengdu. Relax and recover with a soothing Sichuan hotpot, or explore any attractions you may have missed at the beginning of your journey. The drive back to Chengdu takes about 3-4 hours from Siguniang Mountain.

🚗 From Siguniang to Chengdu

  • 🚗Private Car: 3-4 hours

Travel Tips for Route 4

⛰️ Altitude Sickness

Siguniang reaches over 5,000m! Take it slow on first day, drink lots of water, avoid alcohol. Consider bringing altitude sickness medication.

🥾 Hiking Preparation

Bring proper hiking boots, warm layers, rain gear, sunscreen, and plenty of water. Mountain weather changes quickly!

🌡️ Best Time to Visit

May-June: flowers bloom. September-October: golden autumn foliage, clearest views. December-February: snow, great for winter scenery.

🚗 Getting Around

For mountain areas, hiring a private driver is the most convenient option. Public buses are available but less flexible.

🧗 Mountain Safety

If hiking beyond the main scenic areas, hire a local guide. Check weather forecasts. Tell someone your plans before heading out.
