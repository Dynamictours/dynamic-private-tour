Route Itinerary

Day 1-2

Chengdu - Home of Giant Pandas

China's culinary capital and gateway to Tibet. Visit the world-famous Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding, explore ancient Jinli Street, and indulge in authentic Sichuan hotpot, mapo tofu, and kung pao chicken. Don't miss the laid-back Chengdu lifestyle with its famous teahouses and mahjong culture.

Nearby Attraction: Bipenggou

Note: Bipenggou is a beautiful alpine valley located about 4-5 hours from Chengdu by car. Known for its snow-capped mountains, turquoise lakes, and colorful autumn foliage. It's a perfect side trip from Chengdu for nature lovers!

Where to Stay in Chengdu

  • Chunxi Road / Taikoo Li
    City center, best for shopping
    ¥350-1500/night
  • Kuanzhai Alley Area
    Historic district, local flavor

  • Jinjiang District

💡 Visit the Panda Base early in the morning (7:30 AM) when pandas are most active. Get there before 9 AM for the best viewing experience!

Day 3-4

Bipenggou - The Hidden Gem of Sichuan

A stunning alpine valley located in Aba Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, about 200km northwest of Chengdu. Famous for its dramatic mountain scenery, pristine lakes, waterfalls, and Tibetan culture. Best visited in autumn (October) for golden foliage or winter for snow activities.

🚌 From Chengdu to Bipenggou

  • 🚗Private Car/Taxi: 4-5 hours (¥300-500 one-way)
  • 🚌Bus from Chadianzi Station: 4-5 hours (¥60-80)
  • 🚐Join a day tour: most convenient option

What to See in Bipenggou

🏞️ Highlights: Napa Lake (娜姆湖), Princess Lake (女皇峰), Alpine Meadows, Ancient Bipenggou Waterfalls, Tibetan Villages

⛰️ Altitude: 2,000-5,000 meters above sea level

🌤️ Best Time: October (autumn colors), December-February (skiing)

Where to Stay in Bipenggou

  • Bipenggou Scenic Area
    Inside the park, limited options
    ¥300-800/night
  • Li County (Lixian)
    30 mins from entrance
    ¥200-500/night

💡 Bring warm clothing even in summer! Temperatures drop significantly at higher altitudes. Consider altitude sickness precautions.

Day 5-7

Chongqing - The Mountain City

The famous "3D city" built on mountains with stunning night views, spicy hotpot, and unique stilt house architecture. Take the famous Hongyadong (Hongya Cave), walk through ancient Ciqikou Ancient Town, and ride the famous Liziba Metro Station that goes through a building!

🚄 From Chengdu to Chongqing

  • 🚄High-Speed Train: 1.5-2 hours (¥154-468)

(From Bipenggou, return to Chengdu first (4-5 hours), then take high-speed train to Chongqing.

Where to Stay in Chongqing

  • Hongyadong Area

    ¥400-1800/night
  • Ciqikou Ancient Town

💡 Book a hotel with river views for the best night views of Chongqing's famous skyline. Don't miss the cruise on Yangtze River!

Day 7-8

Return to Chengdu

Complete your Sichuan adventure by returning to Chengdu. Enjoy one last Sichuan hotpot meal or do some last-minute shopping for tea and local specialties before your departure.

🚄 From Chongqing to Chengdu

  • 🚄High-Speed Train: 1.5-2 hours (¥154-468)

Travel Tips for Route 3

🌶️ Spice Level

Sichuan and Chongqing food is NUMBING SPICY (ma la). Ask for "wei la" (mild spicy) if you're not used to it. Always have milk or yogurt ready!

💰 Budget

Mid-range budget: ¥700-1300/day. Hotpot meals ¥80-200/person. Bipenggou entrance ¥80 + shuttle bus ¥60.

🏔️ Altitude in Bipenggou

Bipenggou reaches 3,000m+. Take it slow on first day, drink plenty of water, avoid alcohol.

🐼 Panda Visiting Tips

Visit Chengdu Panda Base early morning 7:30 AM. Avoid weekends and holidays. Book tickets online.

🚗 Getting to Bipenggou

Best way: join a day tour from Chengdu or hire a private driver. Public buses available but less convenient.

🌡️ Best Time

Autumn (September-October: best weather. Spring: March-April: cherry blossoms. Winter: December-February: snow in Bipenggou.
