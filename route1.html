Route Itinerary

Day 1-3

Shanghai - The Modern Metropolis

China's largest city and financial hub. Explore the iconic Bund skyline, traditional Yu Garden, and trendy French Concession. Experience the perfect blend of modern skyscrapers and historical architecture.

Where to Stay

  • The Bund / Nanjing Road
    City center, close to landmarks
    ¥500-1500/night
  • French Concession
    Trendy cafes, boutique hotels
    ¥400-1200/night
  • Pudong (Lujiazui)
    Luxury hotels, skyline views
    ¥800-3000/night

💡 Stay near The Bund or People's Square for easy access to major attractions and metro lines.

Day 4-6

Beijing - The Imperial Capital

China's capital city with 3,000 years of history. Visit the magnificent Forbidden City, walk the Great Wall of China, and explore the beautiful Temple of Heaven. Experience authentic Peking duck and hutong culture.

🚄 From Shanghai to Beijing

  • 🚄High-Speed Train: 4.5-5.5 hours (¥553-1748)
  • ✈️Flight: 2 hours (¥400-1200)

Where to Stay

  • Wangfujing / Dongdan
    Central, near Forbidden City
    ¥500-1500/night
  • Nanluoguxiang (Hutong Area)
    Traditional courtyard hotels
    ¥300-800/night
  • Chaoyang District
    Modern, good for nightlife
    ¥400-1200/night

💡 Book train tickets in advance (2 weeks) for Beijing trips, especially during peak seasons.

Day 7-8

Suzhou - The Venice of the East

Famous for its classical Chinese gardens, ancient canals, and silk production. Visit the UNESCO-listed Humble Administrator's Garden, take a boat ride through Pingjiang Road canals, and learn about traditional silk weaving.

🚄 From Beijing to Suzhou

  • 🚄High-Speed Train: 4-5 hours (¥523-1648)
  • ✈️Flight to Shanghai + Train: 3-4 hours total

Where to Stay

  • Pingjiang Road Historic District
    Canal views, boutique stays
    ¥300-800/night
  • Suzhou Industrial Park
    Modern, good for business
    ¥400-1000/night

💡 Suzhou is perfect for day trips from Shanghai (30 mins by train), but staying overnight lets you experience the peaceful evenings.

Day 9-10

Hangzhou - The Paradise on Earth

Marco Polo once called Hangzhou "the finest and noblest city in the world." Known for the stunning West Lake, Longjing tea plantations, and rich cultural heritage. Cycle around the lake, visit Lingyin Temple, and taste authentic Hangzhou cuisine.

🚄 From Suzhou to Hangzhou

  • 🚄High-Speed Train: 1-1.5 hours (¥110-348)

Where to Stay

  • West Lake Area (Youshan Road)
    Scenic views, close to attractions
    ¥500-1500/night
  • Qinghefang Ancient Street
    Historic district, local flavor
    ¥300-700/night
  • Binjiang District
    Modern, good for long stays
    ¥300-800/night

💡 Book a hotel with West Lake view for the best experience. Early morning and evening are the best times to visit the lake.

Day 11-12

Nanjing - The Southern Capital

Former capital of China with rich imperial history. Explore the Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, walk the ancient Nanjing City Wall, and visit the Presidential Palace. Don't miss the beautiful Xuanwu Lake and the memorial sites that tell China's modern history.

🚄 From Hangzhou to Nanjing

  • 🚄High-Speed Train: 1.5-2 hours (¥117-371)

Where to Stay

  • Xinjiekou CBD
    City center, convenient
    ¥350-1200/night
  • Xuanwu Lake Area
    Lake views, peaceful
    ¥400-1000/night
  • Fuzimiao (Confucius Temple)
    Historic district, night markets
    ¥250-600/night

💡 From Nanjing, high-speed trains connect you back to Shanghai in just 1-1.5 hours, making it easy to wrap up your journey.

Day 12

Return to Shanghai

Complete your Eastern China journey by returning to Shanghai, where you can do some last-minute shopping at Nanjing Road or relax before your departure.

🚄 From Nanjing to Shanghai

  • 🚄High-Speed Train: 1-1.5 hours (¥134-429)

Travel Tips for Route 1

🎫 Train Tickets

Book high-speed train tickets 2-4 weeks in advance, especially during peak seasons (Spring Festival, National Day). Use 12306 app for bookings.

💰 Budget

Mid-range budget: ¥800-1500/day per person including accommodation, food, transportation, and attractions.

🌡️ Best Time

Spring (April-May) and Autumn (September-October) offer the most comfortable weather for this route.

🚇 Transportation

All cities have excellent metro systems. Get a transportation card (Yikatong) for seamless travel on buses and subway.

📱 Apps

Download: 12306 (trains), Alipay/WeChat Pay (payments), Didi (ride-hailing), Ctrip (hotels).

🍜 Must-Try Foods

Shanghai: xiaolongbao, Beijing: Peking duck, Suzhou: squirrel fish, Hangzhou: West Lake vinegar fish, Nanjing: salted duck.
